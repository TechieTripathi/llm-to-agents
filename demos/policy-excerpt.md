# Kestrel Freight: Travel and Expenses Policy (excerpt)

Version 3.2 · Effective 1 April 2026 · Owner: Group Finance

> Fictional example data for the "From LLMs to AI Agents" workshop. This is not a real company policy. The excerpt deliberately covers only sections 4.1–4.6; it says nothing about mileage, car hire or weekend overtime.

## 4. Business travel

**4.1 Approval.** Overnight trips must be approved by your line manager before anything is booked.

**4.2 Booking.** Book all rail, air and hotel travel through TravelDesk, the company travel portal. Bookings made elsewhere are not reimbursed unless TravelDesk cannot serve the trip and Group Finance has agreed in writing.

**4.3 Class of travel.** Rail travel is standard class. Flights under 6 hours are economy class.

**4.4 Hotels.** Hotel costs are reimbursed up to €130 per night including breakfast, or €160 per night in capital cities.

**4.5 Meals.** When you stay overnight, actual meal costs are reimbursed up to €40 per day against itemised receipts. There is no daily allowance (per diem). Alcohol is not reimbursed.

**4.6 Claims.** Submit claims in the expenses system within 60 days of the trip. Attach an itemised receipt for every item claimed.
